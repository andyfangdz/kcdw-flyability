How reliable are the gust probabilities?

Research notes · What the model learned

For each threshold, the chance that the hour's 1-minute peak gust at KCDW reaches it, from the 7 a.m. update for that day and the seven after. A reliable forecast sits on the diagonal: of all the hours given a 30% chance, about 30% should see the gust. Every point is out of sample (month-interleaved cross-validation, 214,856 hours from 2021 to 2026); bars are 90% intervals counted in days, since windy hours cluster on the same days. Points from fewer than three days are not drawn.
